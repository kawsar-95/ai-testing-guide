# Meeting Intelligence

A tester sits in many meetings: bug triage, sprint planning, and requirement talks. Meeting intelligence tools record these meetings, write them down, and summarize them, so the facts that you need for a test are not lost.

<PageMeta />

## In plain words

<Voices>
<Technical>

**Meeting intelligence** is the use of AI to capture a meeting and turn it into text that you can search and reuse. A typical tool records the call, makes a transcript, and writes a summary. Gong, for example, records, transcribes, and analyzes calls <Cite n={1} />. Its call summary has a recap, key points, and next steps <Cite n={1} />.

The skill is not only to press "record". The skill is to ask the right questions of the transcript afterwards: "Which acceptance criteria did the product owner agree to?", "Which bugs did we decide to fix in this release?", "Who owns each action?"

For a tester, a meeting is a source of requirements. A decision that only lives in someone's memory is hard to test. A decision in a searchable transcript is evidence.

</Technical>
<Layman>

## How it works

<Voices>
<Technical>

Most tools join the call as a bot participant. Fireflies first asks for access to the meeting. After that, it is listed with the other people in the call and writes notes <Cite n={4} />. In Google Meet, Fireflies can enter only after the host lets it in <Cite n={4} />. Fathom records calls on Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet only when you are also on the call, and it leaves when the meeting ends <Cite n={3} />.

```text
meeting audio --> notetaker bot --> transcript --> AI summary
                                        |              |
                                   search words   recap, key points,
                                                  next steps
```

After the call, you work with the output. In Gong, you can search the transcript for words or phrases <Cite n={1} />, and Gong can find patterns across many calls <Cite n={1} />. It also highlights key topics, objections, and commitments <Cite n={1} />.

Recording has rules. Gong offers a consent page that can ask people for consent before a web call is recorded <Cite n={2} />, and it can also notify people with an audio prompt and a pre-call email <Cite n={2} />. For phone calls, you must tell the other side out loud that the call is recorded <Cite n={2} />. Gong's help says that before you record, you must understand and follow the consent laws that apply <Cite n={2} />.

</Technical>

## How a tester uses it

<Voices>
<Technical>

- **Bug triage calls.** Record the triage meeting. Afterwards, search the transcript for each bug ID and copy the decision (fix now, defer, or reject) into the ticket. The summary's next steps <Cite n={1} /> become a checklist that you can follow up on.
- **Requirement meetings.** Ask the AI to list every sentence that sounds like a rule: "must", "should", "only if", "never". Turn each rule into a draft acceptance criterion, then confirm it with the product owner. The [Prompting](/ai-skills/prompting/) page shows how to write that request.
- **Find gaps in acceptance criteria.** Search for words such as "edge case", "what if", or "later" <Cite n={1} />. They often mark open questions that nobody wrote down.
- **Patterns over time.** If the same complaint shows up in many calls, it may point to a weak area of the product. Gong can look for patterns across calls <Cite n={1} />. Feed that into your risk-based test plan.

<Callout type="tip" title="Tutorial advice">
This is the tutorial's own advice. Treat an AI summary as a draft, not as the record. Before you write a test from it, check the sentence in the transcript, and ask the speaker when the words are not clear.
</Callout>

</Technical>

- **Bug triage calls.** Record the triage meeting. Later, search the transcript for each bug ID. Copy the decision (fix now, fix later, or reject) into the ticket. The next steps in the summary <Cite n={1} /> become a checklist.

- **বাগ ট্রায়াজ কল।** ট্রায়াজ মিটিং রেকর্ড করুন। পরে প্রতিটা বাগ ID দিয়ে ট্রান্সক্রিপ্টে সার্চ করুন। সিদ্ধান্তটা (এখনই ঠিক করা, পরে করা, বা বাতিল) টিকেটে কপি করুন। সারাংশের পরের ধাপগুলো <Cite n={1} /> একটা চেকলিস্ট হয়ে যায়।
- **রিকোয়ারমেন্ট মিটিং।** AI-কে বলুন নিয়মের মতো শোনায় এমন প্রতিটা বাক্য তালিকা করতে। "must", "should", "only if", "never" খুঁজুন। প্রতিটা নিয়মকে একটা খসড়া acceptance criterion বানান। তারপর প্রোডাক্ট ওনারের সাথে মিলিয়ে নিন। কীভাবে চাইবেন তা [Prompting](/ai-skills/prompting/) পেজে আছে।
- **Acceptance criteria-র ফাঁক খুঁজুন।** "edge case", "what if" বা "later" জাতীয় শব্দ সার্চ করুন <Cite n={1} />। এগুলো প্রায়ই এমন খোলা প্রশ্ন দেখায়, যা কেউ লিখে রাখেনি।

এটা টিউটোরিয়ালের নিজের পরামর্শ। AI-এর সারাংশ একটা খসড়া, আসল রেকর্ড নয়। এ থেকে টেস্ট লেখার আগে ট্রান্সক্রিপ্টে বাক্যটা মিলিয়ে নিন। কথা পরিষ্কার না হলে বক্তাকে জিজ্ঞেস করুন।

## Common mistakes

<Voices>
<Technical>

- **Recording without consent.** Consent rules depend on where the people are, and a tool's help can tell you to follow them before you record <Cite n={2} />. Fix: use the consent features of the tool and say at the start that the call is recorded.
- **Trusting the summary blindly.** A summary can drop a condition such as "only for admin users". Fix: check each rule against the transcript before it goes into a test case.
- **Recording everything forever.** Transcripts can hold customer names and internal plans. Fix: follow your company's retention rules, and see [AI Governance](/ai-skills/ai-governance/).
- **No owner for the output.** Notes that nobody reads change nothing. Fix: one person moves the decisions into tickets on the same day.

</Technical>
<Layman>

- **Recording without consent.** Consent rules depend on where people are. A tool's help can tell you to follow them before you record <Cite n={2} />. Fix: use the consent features of the tool. Say at the start that the call is recorded.
- **Trusting the summary blindly.** A summary can drop a condition, such as "only for admin users". Fix: check each rule in the transcript before it goes into a test case.
- **Keeping recordings forever.** Transcripts can hold customer names and private plans. Fix: follow the keep-and-delete rules of your company. See [AI Governance](/ai-skills/ai-governance/).
- **Nobody owns the notes.** Notes that nobody reads change nothing. Fix: one person moves the decisions into tickets on the same day.

</Layman>
<Bangla>

- **সম্মতি ছাড়া রেকর্ড করা।** সম্মতির নিয়ম নির্ভর করে মানুষ কোথায় আছে তার উপর। টুলের হেল্প রেকর্ডের আগে সেগুলো মানতে বলতে পারে <Cite n={2} />। সমাধান: টুলের consent ফিচার ব্যবহার করুন। শুরুতেই বলুন যে কল রেকর্ড হচ্ছে।
- **চোখ বন্ধ করে সারাংশে ভরসা করা।** সারাংশে "শুধু admin ইউজারের জন্য" জাতীয় শর্ত বাদ পড়তে পারে। সমাধান: টেস্ট কেসে নেওয়ার আগে প্রতিটা নিয়ম ট্রান্সক্রিপ্টে মিলিয়ে নিন।

## How to start

<Voices>
<Technical>

1. Ask your team lead which notetaker, if any, your company allows.
2. Pick one recurring meeting, such as the weekly bug triage, and tell the attendees that you will record it. Use the tool's consent notice if it has one <Cite n={2} />.
3. After the meeting, read the summary next to the transcript. Note one thing the summary missed.
4. Write a prompt that pulls out decisions and rules, and reuse it after every meeting.
5. Move each decision into its ticket, and link the transcript time stamp.

</Technical>
<Layman>

1. Ask your team lead which notetaker your company allows, if any.
2. Pick one meeting that repeats, such as the weekly bug triage. Tell the people that you will record it. Use the consent notice of the tool if it has one <Cite n={2} />.
3. After the meeting, read the summary next to the transcript. Note one thing that the summary missed.
4. Write a prompt that pulls out decisions and rules. Use it again after every meeting.
5. Move each decision into its ticket. Link the time in the transcript.

</Layman>

1. আপনার টিম লিডকে জিজ্ঞেস করুন কোম্পানি কোন নোটটেকার অনুমোদন করে, যদি করে।
