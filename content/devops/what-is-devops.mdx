# What Is DevOps?

What DevOps is, and how it joins development and operations. A short explanation with its sources comes first. The reels after it show the topic at work.

<PageMeta />

## What it is

<Voices>
<Technical>

DevOps is the combination of cultural philosophies, practices, and tools that increases an organization's ability to deliver applications and services at high velocity <Cite n={1} />. DevOps combines development (Dev) and operations (Ops) to unite people, process, and technology in application planning, development, delivery, and operations <Cite n={2} />. DevOps enables coordination and collaboration between formerly siloed roles like development, IT operations, quality engineering, and security <Cite n={2} />.

</Technical>
<Layman>

DevOps is a mix of ways of thinking, ways of working, and tools. It helps an organization deliver applications and services at high speed <Cite n={1} />. DevOps joins development (Dev) and operations (Ops). It brings people, process, and technology together in application planning, development, delivery, and operations <Cite n={2} />. Roles such as development, IT operations, quality engineering, and security used to work apart. DevOps lets them coordinate and work together <Cite n={2} />.

</Layman>
<Bangla>

DevOps হলো চিন্তার ধরন, কাজের ধরন আর tool-এর একটা মিশ্রণ। এটা একটা organization-কে খুব দ্রুত application ও service deliver করতে সাহায্য করে <Cite n={1} />। DevOps development (Dev) আর operations (Ops)-কে একসাথে জোড়ে। এটা application planning, development, delivery ও operations-এ মানুষ, process আর technology-কে এক করে <Cite n={2} />। development, IT operations, quality engineering ও security-র মতো ভূমিকাগুলো আগে আলাদা আলাদা কাজ করত। DevOps তাদের মধ্যে সমন্বয় ও সহযোগিতা সম্ভব করে <Cite n={2} />।
