## What it is

<Voices>
<Technical>

Git thinks of its data as a series of snapshots of a miniature filesystem: each time you commit, Git takes a picture of what all your files look like at that moment <Cite n={1} />. A merge joins two or more development histories together <Cite n={2} />.

</Technical>
