## Wording notes (review round 1)

C27 and C28 are now on the page with their exception and condition. The "Common mistakes" bullet groups only the MUST rules (public clients and PKCE, CSRF defense, exact redirect matching, no password grant) and treats the implicit grant as advice (SHOULD NOT). RFC 9700 lets PKCE or an OpenID Connect nonce replace `state`; the CSRF bullet of the page now says so (C26). The Key words row for the implicit grant says "recommends against". C31 is no longer on the page.
