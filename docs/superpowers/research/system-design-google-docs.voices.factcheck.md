# Voice fact-check: How Google Docs Works (round 1, 2026-10-20)

Page: `content/system-design/google-docs.mdx`. I did not write this page.

Method: I read the 7 `<Voices>` blocks. For each claim in a `<Layman>` or `<Bangla>` panel, I found the `<Technical>` sentence of the same `##` section that it restates. A claim that is not in the Technical panel, or that changes a number, a name, a condition, or a scope, is a FAIL. An omission is not a FAIL. For each `<Cite n>` in a voice panel, I checked that the Technical panel of the same section cites the same `n` and that the cite sits after the same claim. Sources 1 to 4 are the same as in `system-design-google-docs.factcheck.md`. I read the Bangla text for meaning and for natural use, and I checked that common technical terms stay in English and that an English word and a Bangla suffix have a hyphen. The "Key words" section has no voices. The 7 sections with voices are: The problem in plain words, The big picture, Why a lock is too slow, Hiding the wait, Store the edits not the text, Who sends what and when, and What a tester can learn.

Flagged items:

- "must" for "should" (section 1, Layman, row 6): the Technical text says "A person should also be able to keep editing while offline." The Layman text says "must". The strength of the need is a little higher. It is not a number, a name, a scope, or a condition, and the Bangla ("দরকার", needed) is neutral. PASS with a language note.
- "Luiz types IT at the start" (section 5, Layman, row 81): the Technical text says Luiz changes the text to "IT'S EASY AS 123" and "types IT first". "At the start" follows from that target text, and "moves 2 places to the right" in the same Technical text needs it. PASS.
- Attribution dropped (section 5, Layman, rows 79, 83, 84): "the newsletter calls OT optimistic", "Google says", and "the newsletter warns" are removed. The sentences keep the same cites ([1], [3], [1]). The claims are the same. PASS.
- Third gain dropped (section 6, Layman, rows 105 and 106): the Technical text lists 3 gains. The Layman text gives 2 of them. An omission, so PASS.
- Analogies: the Technical text has 4 pictures (meeting room, shopping list, bank account, restaurant). They are not claims about the system. The Layman text keeps the meeting room (row 36). The Bangla text keeps the meeting room and the shopping list (rows 44 and 63). Dropping a picture is an omission, so PASS.
- The table cells quote a Technical table with " / " between the two cells, so that the report table keeps its columns (rows 57 to 59 and 68 to 70).
