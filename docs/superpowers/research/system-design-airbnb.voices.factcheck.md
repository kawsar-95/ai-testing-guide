Method: I read the 6 `<Voices>` blocks. For each claim in a `<Layman>` or `<Bangla>` panel, I found the `<Technical>` sentence of the same `##` section that it restates. A claim that is not in the Technical panel, or that changes a number, a name, a condition, or a scope, is a FAIL. An omission is not a FAIL. Each `<Cite n>` in a voice panel is `n={1}`. Each Technical panel with a cite also cites source 1, and the page has one source only (the article). I checked that each voice cite follows a claim that the Technical panel also cites to source 1. The "Key words" section has no voices. I read the Bangla text for meaning and natural use, and I checked that technical terms stay in English, that the hyphen joins an English word and a Bangla suffix, and that the address form is polite.
