Method: I read the 6 `<Voices>` blocks. For each claim in a `<Layman>` or `<Bangla>` panel, I found the sentence of the `<Technical>` panel of the same `##` section and the same version that it restates. A `<Cite>` in a notes block is a FAIL: there is none (script check). For each `<Cite n>` in an istqb voice block, I checked that the Technical text cites the same n for the same claim, and that the claim agrees with the verdict of `fundamentals-lifecycles.factcheck.md` (`Open FAILs: 0`) for that source. I read the Bangla text for meaning and natural use, and I checked that testing terms stay in English, with a hyphen between an English word and a Bangla suffix, and that the text uses the polite form (আপনি).

Mixing check: the notes voices keep the notes warnings of the Technical callouts, with no cites. The istqb voices keep the ISTQB statements with the same cites. I found no ISTQB claim in a notes block and no notes claim presented as ISTQB fact in an istqb block.
