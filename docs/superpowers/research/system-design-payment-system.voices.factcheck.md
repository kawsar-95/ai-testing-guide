Method: I read the 8 `<Voices>` blocks. For each claim in a `<Layman>` or `<Bangla>` panel, I found the `<Technical>` sentence of the same `##` section that it restates. The Technical facts are the subject of the fact-check of the page itself. I read the Bangla text for meaning and for natural use. I checked that common technical terms stay in English and that a hyphen joins an English word and a Bangla suffix. The "Key words" section has no voices. The "What a tester can learn" section has voices. Its Technical panel is the tutorial's own advice.
