## Not on the page

The page gives no attack steps. It names assertion injection and replay only as the risks that the checks (audience, nonce, assertion id, short validity) are for. The page gives no number except those in the sources (none). The example (a test tool and a bug tracker), the tester checks, and the "same idea, different names" comparison are the tutorial's own text and have no cite. The page does not say that OIDC or SAML "is" SSO: NIST says federation "is sometimes known as" single sign-on, and the page keeps that hedge. The page does not cover OAuth 2.0 authorization; it links to `/course/oauth-oidc/`.

## Changes after review (2026-10-22)

- C8 (trust agreement): the page no longer says the parties "first agree". It says they "work under" a trust agreement, because NIST lets the agreement be set up during the transaction in some cases. The quote of C8 stands.
- C24 (assertion contents): the page says an assertion "usually has" a subject. S2: "usually contains a subject of the assertion".
- C27 and C28 (single logout, Key words row): the row says "at nearly the same time, with no guarantee". S2: "near-simultaneous logout" and "the result of the logout process cannot be guaranteed".
