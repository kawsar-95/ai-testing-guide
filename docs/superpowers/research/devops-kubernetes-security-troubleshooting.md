Page: `content/devops/kubernetes-security-troubleshooting.mdx`. Module: DevOps & Cloud. Date: 2026-11-18 (first version: 2026-11-05).

The page is a full lesson for a tester (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-15-devops-full-lessons-design.md`): Secrets and how they are stored, who may do what (RBAC and service accounts), which Pods may run (Pod Security admission), which Pods may talk to which (NetworkPolicy), and the commands that show why a Pod fails, with a practice run on the kind cluster of `kubernetes-basics`. The reels after the text are another creator's videos and are not sources. Each fact in the text comes from the sources below. The first version of the page (2026-11-05) had two sentences from the Secrets page; the page keeps them in `## What it is`.
